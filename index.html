Chapter 1: Tuesday, Nov 24

Chicago to South Indianapolis / Greenwood

~3 hrs / 185 mi CT to ET (-1 hr on clock) Easy Recovery Night
  • 2:00 to 4:00 PM CT Wrap up the Deljo furnace check at 2550 W Logan Blvd (grab the paid itemized receipt for closing).
  • ~4:15 PM CT Load the pillows, blankets, and Winston into the car and roll south on I-65 S.
  • ~8:15 PM ET Arrive in South Indianapolis / Greenwood (right on I-65 S, saving 20 minutes off Wednesday morning!). Check in, grab easy comfort food, and get cozy.

Verified 70 lb Dog Stays (Tue Nov 24, Budget Picks)

  • Best Western Plus Greenwood (I-65 Exit 99): Top budget pick right at Exit 99! 4.2 stars, 80 lb max, $25 pet fee, free breakfast (~$116 OTA / ~$144 direct all-in).
  • La Quinta Columbus-Edinburgh (I-65 Exit 76B): 25 min further south (trims Day 2!). Clean 4.2 stars, verified 75 lb max, $25 pet fee, free hot breakfast (~$116 OTA / ~$125 direct w/ free cancel).
  • Staybridge Suites Greenwood (I-65 Exit 99): Suite upgrade pick (4.5 stars, verified <80 lbs, $75 fee, full kitchen & free hot breakfast, ~$206 to $222 all-in).

Rest Stops on Leg 1 (I-65 S)

  • Indiana I-65 Official Rest Areas (Kankakee / Lebanon): Clean restrooms plus dedicated grassy pet-relief patches for Winston.
  • Fair Oaks Farms (I-65 Exit 220): Fun halfway stretch with wide perimeter lawns for Winston (note: pets stay outside buildings, easy tag-team coffee/restroom stop).

Easy Dinner Ideas (Greenwood)

  • Oaken Barrel Brewing Co.: Cozy Indiana comfort food right near I-65 Exit 99 Greenwood (easy takeout or relaxed booth).
  • Revery (Old Town Greenwood): Warm modern American dining if feeling up to a nice sit-down meal.
  • Or effortless DoorDash / hotel room picnic in pajamas so Janie can put her feet up immediately!
Chapter 2: Wednesday, Nov 25

Historic Downtown Franklin, Tennessee

~4.5 hrs / 290 mi ET to CT (+1 hr gained!) Main St Boutiques OPEN
  • 9:00 AM ET Cruise south down I-65 S past Louisville and Bowling Green.
  • ~12:30 PM CT Cross back into Central Time (instant bonus hour!) and arrive right on Historic Downtown Franklin Main Street (20 min south of Nashville, zero big-city parking stress).
  • 12:30 to 5:30 PM CT Relaxed, flat brick-sidewalk strolling through cute local boutiques while everything is wide open ahead of Thanksgiving.
  • Evening Cozy dinner on Main Street (or free hot Kickback dinner & drinks at Drury Plaza Franklin!).
Why Franklin beats downtown Nashville for recovery day: Completely flat sidewalks, easy parking, Hallmark-movie charm, and Franklin is a certified Better Cities for Pets town (look for green paw-print stickers on boutique doors welcoming Winston right inside!).

Cute Dog-Friendly Boutiques

  • Finnley's Good Findings: Chic clothing and accessories right on Main St (famously dog-friendly).
  • Hester & Cook: Gorgeous table linens, paper goods, and hostess gifts (pets welcome inside!).
  • Anthropologie (Historic Building): One of the prettiest Anthropologie spaces in the South, welcoming leashed pups.
  • Walton's Antique Jewelry & Bink's Outfitters: Warm local favorites that love meeting friendly dogs.

Coffee & Cozy Restaurants

  • Frothy Monkey: All-day cafe inside a historic church manse with a spacious dog-friendly patio.
  • Merridee's Breadbasket: Iconic Franklin scratch bakery (cinnamon rolls, soup, sandwiches, outdoor seating).
  • McGavock's Coffee Bar: Streetside coffee and pastries at The Harpeth Hotel.
  • Dinner picks: Puckett's Grocery (Southern comfort + live music), Americana Taphouse (creekside shaded patio), McCreary's Irish Pub, or JJ's Wine Bar.

Rest Stops, Walks & Hotels

  • On the way (I-65 S): Kentucky Welcome Centers + Buc-ee's Smiths Grove, KY (Exit 38: spotless restrooms & road snacks; pets stay outside on perimeter grass!).
  • Winston zoomies: The Park at Harlinsdale Farm (K-9 Korral) (5 min from Main St, huge fenced dog park!).
  • Verified 70 lb dog stays (Wed Nov 25): Candlewood Suites Franklin (4.5 stars, only 2.2 mi to Main St, 80 lb max, ~$167 OTA / ~$192 direct all-in), Tru by Hilton Cool Springs (4.5 stars, big dogs OK, free breakfast, ~$183 OTA all-in), or Drury Plaza Franklin (4.7 stars, 80 lb combined, ~$206 OTA / ~$244 member direct with FREE hot dinner, drinks & breakfast!).
Chapter 3A: Thanksgiving Morning, Nov 26

Chattanooga Mountain & River Stroll (~3 Hours)

~2h 10m from Franklin CT to ET (-1 hr on clock) 11:10 AM to 2:00 PM ET
  • 8:00 AM CT Depart Franklin on super light Thanksgiving morning roads along I-24 E through the Cumberland Plateau.
  • ~11:10 AM ET Arrive right by Chattanooga's riverfront as late-autumn sunshine warms up the valley.
  • 11:10 AM to 2:00 PM ET Nearly 3 unhurried hours to walk the newly restored Walnut Street Bridge, explore Bluff View & Coolidge Park, and preview our future favorite 2-hour weekend getaway!
  • 2:00 PM ET Head south down I-75 S toward Fayetteville & Trilith Guesthouse (~2h 10m).
Honest Thanksgiving Day Heads-Up: Independent boutiques and local coffee bars along Frazier Ave and Bluff View close on Thanksgiving Day. The upside? The bridge, parks, and river bluffs are magically peaceful and crowd-free for window-shopping and walking Winston!
Open Chattanooga Walk & Views in Google Maps

Best Flat, Scenic Walking Spots

  • Walnut Street Pedestrian Bridge: Just reopened Oct 3, 2026 after an 18-month restoration! Flat wooden deck with dramatic Tennessee River & mountain views.
  • Bluff View Art District: Ivy-covered cobblestone lanes, outdoor River Gallery Sculpture Garden, and scenic river cliffs (free & open 24/7).
  • Coolidge Park & Renaissance Park: Wide flat grassy riverfront lawns right across the bridge for Winston.
  • Zero-Walking Bonus: Quick 10-min scenic drive up Lookout Mountain / Point Park Overlook right from the car!

NorthShore & Bluff View Shops (Window-Shop + Weekend Bookmarks)

  • Almanac Supply Co. (Frazier Ave): Beloved candles, home goods, and small-batch gifts.
  • Plum Nelly: Whimsical local gifts, art, and home accents.
  • River Gallery (Bluff View): Fine regional art, pottery, and jewelry perched above the river.
  • Frazier Avenue Indie Strip: Vintage shops, bookshops, and boutiques to save for our first Saturday trip back (just 2 hours up I-75 from Peachtree City!).

Thanksgiving Morning Coffee & Stops

  • Open Thanksgiving Morning: Nearby Starbucks or 24/7 downtown staple City Cafe Diner for hot drinks & pastries.
  • Bookmark for Next Visit: Rembrandt's Coffee House (Bluff View), Milk & Honey, and Mean Mug.
  • Rest Stop Heading South (I-75 S): Buc-ee's Calhoun, GA (Exit 310, halfway to Trilith; spotless restrooms & snacks, walk Winston on perimeter grass).
